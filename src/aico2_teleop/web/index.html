How the clutch works

  • Motion is relative. When you squeeze the grip, your hand's pose at that moment is matched to the gripper's current pose, so nothing moves until your hand does.
  • Hand translation is amplified 1.5×; wrist rotation is damped to 0.5×.
  • To reach further than your arm comfortably goes, ratchet: release the grip, bring your hand back, squeeze again, continue.
  • "Forward" is away from you, measured from the way you were facing when you squeezed.
  • Each arm's clutch is independent: you can hold one arm still while moving the other.

Start-up checklist

  1. Both arms powered, released from E-stop, and in the Flexiv app's remote mode.
  2. On the robot PC, one terminal per arm (outside ROS, in noros.sh):
    sudo ~/flexiv_ws/src/aico2_rt_control/standalone/build/rt_server Rizon4-063823 --shm /aico2_rt_left --gripper Flexiv-GN01 --rt-cpu 2 --control-waist
    sudo ~/flexiv_ws/src/aico2_rt_control/standalone/build/rt_server Rizon4R-062102 --shm /aico2_rt_right --gripper Flexiv-GN01 --rt-cpu 3
    Do not wrap these in taskset: --rt-cpu pins only the control loop. Only the LEFT server gets --control-waist; the right one follows the waist.
  3. In a sourced ROS terminal (kept off the RT cores):
    taskset -c 0,1,4-7 ros2 launch aico2_teleop vr.launch.py arm:=both hardware:=true hardware_backend:=rt drive_base:=true
  4. Wait for solver warmed up twice (once per arm) in the launch terminal.
  5. In the headset browser open https://<robot-pc-ip>:8443 (accept the certificate warning the first time).
  6. Press Enter VR, then press any button on each controller to wake it. This page is hidden while in VR; exit VR at any time and the boxes above show the last state of each hand (both should read tracked).
  7. Stand facing the robot, squeeze one grip, and move slowly to confirm direction before working at speed.

Safety

Releasing the grip stops that arm. Keep a hand near the physical E-stop for every session.
  • If input stops for more than 0.15 s (tracking lost, socket dropped, headset removed), the arm halts on its own.
  • The arm refuses commands that would hit itself, the other arm, or a joint limit, and refuses sudden IK branch flips. It holds instead.
  • Don't take the headset off with a grip held.

Troubleshooting

SymptomCause / fix
"NO controllers reported"Controllers asleep: press any button on each.
One box stays "not seen"That controller is off or not paired. Check its battery.
"socket closed, retrying"The launch on the robot PC is not running, or the network dropped. The page reconnects by itself.
Page won't loadWrong IP, or the PC firewall blocks port 8443. The launch terminal prints the exact URL and the ufw command to allow it.
Arm doesn't move with grip heldCheck the launch terminal: teleop mode is off means the arm's RT server isn't in teleop; holding: in collision or refusing a jump mean the pose was refused. Release, move back, re-squeeze.
Arm lags or feels slowIt is speed-limited for safety (rate_limit_scale in the launch command).
